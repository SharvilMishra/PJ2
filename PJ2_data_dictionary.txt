PJ2 Wellness Dataset: Emily Park, 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2025 (365 rows, one row per day)
Emily Park is an invented person (age 35, highly active, inconsistent sleeper).

date                    Day, YYYY-MM-DD
day_of_week             Monday to Sunday
steps                   Steps taken that day
distance_km             Distance walked or run, km
calories_burned         Total calories burned (resting + active)
exercise_minutes        Minutes of deliberate exercise
exercise_type           Weight Training, Dancing, Walking, Running, Yoga or None
avg_heart_rate_bpm      Average heart rate during the day
resting_heart_rate_bpm  Resting heart rate (lower is generally fitter)
hrv_ms                  Heart-rate variability in ms (higher usually means better recovery)
sleep_hours             Hours slept the night ending that morning
deep_sleep_pct          Share of sleep that was deep sleep, %
bedtime / wake_time     24-hour clock, HH:MM
sleep_score             0 to 100 overall sleep quality
stress_score            1 to 10 (higher is more stress). A dashboard can turn this into a label such as Low / Moderate / High
water_liters            Water logged, litres
weight_kg               Morning weight
mood_1to5               Self-reported mood, 1 low to 5 high
